## 5. Notes

- **The baseline is reproducible.** `doc-references.sh` re-run at `777595f` in a detached
  worktree, removed afterwards, matched `/tmp/us007-baseline/doc-references.before.txt` byte for
  byte. The walk's close-time identity diff (609 rows each side) had no `<` or `>` line, but it
  predated `../18-TESTS/AUTOMATED/US007-TEST-STATUS.md`. Re-read at the `23-pr-and-review` final
  pass, the diff has no `<` line and one `>` line, from that record's generated block (F-014).
  The other four gates are identical to their baselines bar the run timestamp.
- **The base moved.** The branch was cut at `777595f` and rebased onto `a9378ec` (PR #19, the
  Rust advisory sweep) before its first commit. PR #19 touched six files outside the edit set and
  moved no `doc-references.sh` finding.
- **No register entry was closed here.** `GAPS.md`'s entry of 01/09/2026 (the two competing
  story-status vocabularies) was marked `✅ CLOSED 31/10/2026` by the 7.7.0 change. It was then
  removed on 01/11/2026 by `a8cda1f`, when US007 claimed it (`.claude/CLAUDE.md` Section 9).
  The re-cut's residue edits retire what remained of that claim, so no claim is left unretired
  and nothing goes back to the register. At the walk, the one new `GAPS.md` entry (F-003, F-004)
  was about ClickUp neutrality, which no story owns, and `DEFERRED.md` was unchanged. The
  `23-pr-and-review` final pass added three `DEFERRED.md` rows under a US007 heading (F-007 to
  US008 and US011, F-013 to US001) and a second `GAPS.md` entry of 02/11/2026 (F-015 to F-018).
- **One transient.** A single `lint.sh --path` run on `US000-MANUAL-TESTING.md` exited 3 during
  the walk. Two re-runs exited 0 with no issues. It is recorded here and nowhere else, because
  it did not reproduce.
- **The walk's independence is of context, not of model.** The tester and the build session
  are the same model; Sam's countersignature closes CLOSE-01's intent.
- **The review record is `23-pr-and-review`'s.** `../19-REVIEWS/CLAUDE.md` routes reviews to
  workflow 23 Step 6, so this closeout did not write one.
