## Destination

A project can take a client's questionnaire answers or meeting notes into a **traced requirements
register** (`REQ-###`) before its first feature map, through `workflows/00-client-requirements/`
into `src/00-CLIENT-REQUIREMENTS/`. It can also record **every client comment** from staging review
or after release, each routed to the artefact that acts on it, through `workflows/25-client-feedback/`
into `src/24-CLIENT-FEEDBACK/`. `01`, `23` and `24` route to both.

## Fog of war

- **F-01 — Brand voice before Step 8.** An initial questionnaire is client-facing copy under
  `BRAND-VOICE`, yet it may be sent before setup Step 8 settles the voice, or even before the Step 7
  brief. N-006 and N-009 together would sharpen it.
- **F-02 — One initial questionnaire or a family.** Discovery, GDPR controller, launch volume: one
  document or several, and whether `BLANK/` needs a naming scheme apart from
  `QUESTIONNAIRE-<SLUG>-DD-MM-YYYY`.
- **F-03 — Sent but not returned.** Whether this needs a visible state (folder, status line, or the
  `GAPS.md` block the skill writes today).
- **F-04 — Return format.** Clients may answer in Markdown, Word, PDF or email, and line-level
  `REQ-###` citation depends on which.
- **F-05 — Adjacent stale counts.** `workflows/CONTEXT.md:30-31` "Two things … Steps 7–8" against
  `.claude/CLAUDE.md` 2.2's "Four things", `scale-planning:36` "Step 8", `scale-planning:130`
  "task" node. These are fixed if S-01 edits the same lines.
- **F-06 — Code-layer pairing for `25`.** A bug verdict lands in `21-BUGS`, which borders the
  "_no PM workflow_ → `09`/`10`" pairing rows. Whether `25` gets a pairing row or stays PM-only.
- **F-07 — `.claude/CLAUDE.md` over the ratchet.** Section 2.2 sits past 270 lines, so any change
  to the once-per-project count needs a dated allowance or must be routed out. This only arises
  if N-006 changes the setup step count.
- **F-08 — Source sub-folder layout.** For example `SOURCES/MEETINGS/` and
  `SOURCES/QUESTIONNAIRES/`; each needs a pair (`template-orphans`, `docs-pairing`).
- **F-09 — Generated-repo visibility.** A public or private generated project changes how deep
  redaction must go.
- **F-10 — Is client contact data a processing activity** that `09-GDPR` must record?
- **F-11 — A REQ declined at intake.** Out of the product's scope at intake, mirroring `25`'s
  "declined", and where that reason lives.
