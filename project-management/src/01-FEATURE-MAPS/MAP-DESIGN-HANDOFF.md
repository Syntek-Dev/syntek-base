## Destination

A project names its external design tools at generation (`none` means internal only). The
consolidated set is handed to the chosen tools only after the internal tier has passed. Nothing
external is ever a source, a gate or the record, and whatever comes back is registered and ported
by hand. _(Confirmed by Sam, this conversation, 15/10/2026 (Q14).)_
