\brandsection{Avatars \& Feedback}

Identity markers, progress indicators, and loading placeholders. Avatars carry initials in a rounded square; progress and skeleton components share the same bar primitive at different fills.

\subhead{Avatars}

A single avatar renders the member's initials on a coloured tile.

\vspace{2mm}
\uiavatar[accent]{SB}
\vspace{2mm}

{\small\color{muted}Single avatar, accent tile with initials.}

\vspace{4mm}

Stacked into a group row, avatars summarise a team; the trailing muted tile counts the overflow.

\vspace{2mm}
\uiavatar[primary]{AB}\uiavatar[secondary]{CD}\uiavatar[accent]{EF}\uiavatar[muted]{+3}
\vspace{2mm}

{\small\color{muted}Avatar group -- three members plus a '+3' overflow tile.}

\vspace{4mm}

Beside a name and role, the avatar anchors a member list row.

\subhead{Progress}

Progress bars compose a filled accent segment against a panel remainder; the two widths always sum to the same track length.

\vspace{2mm}
\noindent\uibar{accent}{60}\uibar{panel}{40}\ \ {\small\color{muted}60\%}\par
\vspace{2mm}
\noindent\uibar{accent}{90}\uibar{panel}{10}\ \ {\small\color{muted}90\%}\par

\vspace{2mm}
{\small\color{muted}Three fills across a 100mm track. Pair every bar with a numeric label for screen readers.}
