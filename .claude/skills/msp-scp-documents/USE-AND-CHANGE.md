# Required Sections — Acceptable Use & Change Management

Required section lists for the acceptable use policy and change management policy.
Every document also carries the standard header, version history, ISO alignment note,
and disclaimer defined in [STANDARDS.md](STANDARDS.md).

---

## Acceptable Use Policy

1. Document header (version, owner, status, review date)
2. Version history
3. Purpose and Scope
4. Policy Statement
5. Scope of Coverage (who is bound: employees, contractors, third parties, BYOD users)
6. Acceptable Uses
   - Internet and web browsing
   - Email and messaging
   - Company systems and cloud platforms
   - Mobile and remote access devices
7. Prohibited Activities (explicit, concrete examples — not vague statements)
   - Prohibited internet activities
   - Prohibited email activities
   - Prohibited device activities
   - Data exfiltration and misuse
8. Personal Use Guidelines (permitted personal use parameters)
9. Monitoring and Privacy Notice
   - What is monitored (internet, email, device activity)
   - Legal basis for monitoring (UK GDPR Article 6)
   - Employee rights regarding monitoring
10. Email Policy
11. Internet and Web Browsing Policy
12. Mobile Device and Remote Access Policy
13. Social Media Policy
14. Disciplinary Procedures and Enforcement
15. Related Documents
16. Approval and Sign-Off

> Prohibited activities **must** be concrete, worked examples — not vague statements.
> The monitoring section must cite the UK GDPR Article 6 legal basis; cross-check
> `project-management/docs/GDPR-GUIDE.md`.

---

## Change Management Policy

1. Document header (version, owner, status, review date)
2. Version history
3. Purpose and Scope (which systems and infrastructure are in scope)
4. Policy Statement
5. Change Classification
   - **Standard change** — pre-approved, low risk, well-understood procedure; examples
   - **Normal change** — requires submission, risk assessment, and CAB approval; examples
   - **Emergency change** — urgent; bypasses normal approval; mandatory retrospective review; examples
6. Change Request Process
   - Submission requirements (what must be documented before a change request is raised)
   - Risk and impact assessment criteria
   - Approval authority by change type (Standard / Normal / Emergency)
   - Implementation steps (pre-implementation checklist, implementation window, post-check)
   - Rollback procedure (trigger conditions, responsible owner, rollback window)
7. Change Advisory Board (CAB)
   - Composition (roles — do not name individuals)
   - Meeting frequency and quorum
   - Decision-making authority
8. Emergency Change Procedure (step-by-step; retrospective review requirements; timeframe)
9. Post-Implementation Review (PIR)
   - Who conducts the review
   - Timeframe after implementation
   - How outcomes and lessons learnt are recorded
10. Change Freeze Periods (scheduled freezes, e.g. financial year-end, peak trading)
11. Related Documents (Information Security Policy, Incident Response Plan, Network Security Policy)
12. Approval and Sign-Off
