# Required Sections — Data Retention & Sub-Processor Register

Required section lists for the data retention & disposal policy and the sub-processor
register. Both are GDPR-adjacent — cross-check `project-management/docs/GDPR-GUIDE.md`
and `code/workflows/06-gdpr-enforcement/CONTEXT.md`. Every document also carries the
standard header, version history, ISO alignment note, and disclaimer defined in
[STANDARDS.md](STANDARDS.md).

---

## Data Retention & Disposal Policy

1. Document header (version, owner, status, review date)
2. Version history
3. Purpose and Scope (systems, locations, data formats, and data types covered)
4. Policy Statement
5. Legal and Regulatory Basis (table: Regulation / Act | Relevance | Jurisdiction)
6. Data Categories and Retention Periods

   | Data category | Retention period | Legal basis / reference | Disposal method |
   | ------------- | ---------------- | ----------------------- | --------------- |

7. Retention Period Exceptions
   - Litigation hold (legal proceedings in progress)
   - Regulatory investigation hold
   - Statutory extension (where a regulator requires longer retention)
8. Data Disposal and Destruction
   - Digital deletion methods (secure overwrite, cryptographic erasure, NIST SP 800-88 reference)
   - Physical destruction (cross-cut shredding, certified destruction with certificate)
   - Certificate of Destruction requirements
9. Data Backup and Archive Retention (backup retention periods and disposal)
10. Third-Party and Sub-Processor Data Retention (obligations passed to processors)
11. Roles and Responsibilities (Data Owner, IT, all staff)
12. Compliance, Audit, and Annual Review Schedule
13. Related Documents
14. Approval and Sign-Off

---

## Sub-Processor Register

1. Register header (organisation name, DPA reference, version, date, register owner)
2. Introduction — GDPR Article 28 Obligation (why the register is required and who must maintain it)
3. Controller Notification Clause (timeframe and method for notifying data controllers of sub-processor additions, changes, or removals)
4. Sub-Processor Register Table

   | Sub-processor name | Registered country | Processing activity | Data categories processed | Legal basis | Contractual safeguard (SCCs / adequacy decision) | Date added | Date removed |
   | ------------------ | ------------------ | ------------------- | ------------------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------- | ------------ |

5. Sub-Processor Risk Assessment Summary (brief risk rating per sub-processor: Low / Medium / High)
6. Change Log

   | Date | Change type (Add / Amend / Remove) | Sub-processor | Reason | Controller notified on |
   | ---- | ---------------------------------- | ------------- | ------ | ---------------------- |

7. Register Review and Maintenance Schedule
8. Approval and Sign-Off
